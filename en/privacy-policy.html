Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 30, 2026

1. Our approach

RoutineGuard (“the App”) is a local-first macOS app that manages morning routines and timers on your Mac. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, use for advertising, or track personal data through the App.

2. Information stored on your Mac

The App stores the following information on your Mac to provide its features:

  • Routine item names, order, item durations, and the daily protection time
  • The current day's start and completion state and timestamps
  • A salted hash of the recovery code (the recovery code itself is not stored)
  • A random pairing token used for phone access
  • State required for launch-at-login and safety settings

This information is not sent to the developer's server or a third-party analytics service.

3. Local-network communication

If you use phone control, the App runs a local HTTP service on the Mac and uses Bonjour so a phone on the same local network can connect. The routine display state, remaining duration, start and completion requests, and pairing token are communicated between the Mac and phone.

This communication does not pass through the developer's cloud. Its reach depends on your network configuration, so use a trusted network and do not share the pairing URL.

4. Information we do not collect

  • Name, email address, contacts, or location
  • Advertising identifiers, browsing history, or other-app usage history
  • Analytics events, crash analytics, or diagnostic logs sent automatically to the developer
  • Payment information (the App contains no purchase functionality)

The App contains no ads, analytics SDK, account registration, or tracking feature.

5. Processing by Apple

Apple may process Mac App Store distribution, purchase history, standard diagnostic sharing, and related information under Apple's own policies. This is not data collected by the App for the developer. Please consult Apple's privacy policy for details.

6. Review and delete stored data

The App has no developer-hosted account or server data, so no deletion request to the developer is required. While waiting, disable protection and launch at login. Then quit and delete the App. To remove its local data, open Finder, choose Go → Go to Folder, and delete this container:

~/Library/Containers/com.babashunsuke.RoutineGuard

Confirm that you no longer need the safety configuration before deleting it. Settings cannot be restored after the container is removed.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not collect personal information, including age.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or laws change. Material changes will be published on this page, and the last-updated date above will be revised.
